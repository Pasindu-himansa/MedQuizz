MedQuizz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

MedQuizz is a quiz app for medical students. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and batch.
  • Password: stored only as a secure one-way hash. We never see or store your actual password.
  • Quiz activity: the sessions you join or host, your answers, your scores, and any custom question sets you save.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To send one-time verification codes when you register or reset your password. We do not send marketing email.
  • To run quiz sessions and show your results and progress on your account page.

Services we use

MedQuizz relies on these providers to run:

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Hugging Face hosts the app's server.
  • Neon stores the database.
  • Groq generates quiz questions and explanations. The questions in a session (including custom questions you write) are sent to Groq for this; your name and email are not.
  • Gmail (Google) sends verification-code emails to your address.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Google user data

MedQuizz uses the Gmail API only to send verification-code emails from the MedQuizz account. It does not read, store or access any user's Gmail data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and quiz history while your account exists. To have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below and we will remove it.
